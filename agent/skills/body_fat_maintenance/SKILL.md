# Body-fat and maintenance guidance

Use these facts as evidence context. Do not turn a source fact into an individualized prescription.

- Body-fat ranges are presented in five-to-ten-percentage-point increments to simplify discussion and allow for measurement error. [k:0cb800da]
- For women, the 10–15% body-fat range is below the roughly 15% mark at which periods may stop and long-term bone-mass loss and other health effects may occur. [k:0fbb8021]
- For women, individual exceptions exist around the roughly 15% body-fat threshold, but the speaker characterizes living below that level as unhealthy on average. [k:24f7c31b]
- For men, the 10–15% body-fat range is associated with visible abs in most cases. [k:2f91b094]
- Fat loss is suggested in phases of roughly five to seven percentage points of body fat before taking a break. [k:456dba79]
- MRI is described as the most technically accurate body-fat assessment, followed by DEXA; DEXA is usually the better estimate than visual judgment. [k:ac7197c3]
- The body-fat ranges are averages, and water retention can make a person appear less lean than a DEXA result suggests; reducing water can change appearance without fat loss. [k:b0cc284f]
- Men may maintain good health below 10% body fat, while levels below about 7–8% may reduce androgen hormones for most men and impair muscle retention, training, recovery, and sleep. [k:b1669734]
- Very low body fat in women can shut down the menstrual cycle and has associated health consequences. [k:b24d68f8]
- Visual estimates of body fat are approximate because people differ in where they store fat; the listed ranges are guides rather than checklists. [k:f5b3f212]
