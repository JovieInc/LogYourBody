# Mesocycle structure and deloads

Use these facts as evidence context. Do not turn a source fact into an individualized prescription.

- The speaker gives roughly 2–4 repetitions in reserve as an example of training close to failure. [k:049ae1ff]
- Larger workouts with more sets should be spaced farther apart to allow recovery, while smaller workouts with fewer sets can be performed more frequently if recovery permits. [k:0c1aa50c]
- Progression details such as adding repetitions or load are not specified, though increasing load when repetitions exceed the stated range is given as one intuitive response. [k:140beb31]
- The training summary can be used as a checklist to compare a training program against its stated elements. [k:2d9aa0bd]
- For muscle growth, the speaker summarizes training as sets of 5–30 good-technique repetitions, with each repetition taking 2–9 seconds, performed in multiple sets close to failure while the target muscle is the limiting factor; repeat when sufficiently recovered. [k:7263971f]
- Using only one set to failure per muscle group is described as almost always an unnecessarily restrictive approach for effectiveness; multiple sets are favored. [k:7b88241b]
- The speaker illustrates underperformance with a previous bench performance of 225 pounds for 10 repetitions followed by 230 pounds for 7 repetitions, described as not converting to equivalent performance. [k:850ad582]
- The summary does not specify exercise order, a precise per-session volume, or a detailed method for constructing a plan that supports recovery. [k:a0d268d9]
- To judge recovery, compare performance in the corresponding session with the previous week; performance that is stable or higher is presented as indicating readiness to train again. [k:b1e1dc5b]
- The summary does not substantially explain the stimulus-to-fatigue ratio, which the speaker characterizes as a more advanced programming concept. [k:b9d22f33]
- The summary does not define good technique, explain when different repetition durations are appropriate, or specify how to assess recovery. [k:c69752f0]
- The speaker says that training outside 5–30 repetitions per set is outside the recommended range for muscle growth. [k:cc4a8cc4]
- Each repetition is described as taking 2–9 seconds; the speaker suggests reconsidering repetitions lasting about 1 second or about 15 seconds. [k:d51cb3a6]
- Recovery is summarized as performance remaining at least stable and preferably improving over time. [k:dcd79417]
- If a muscle targeted by an exercise is not the limiting factor, performance of that exercise may be constrained by another muscle group. [k:f3a69a7e]
