# Recovery signals

Use these facts as evidence context. Do not turn a source fact into an individualized prescription.

- Systemic fatigue can reduce the ability to train hard in later sessions. [k:0c039419]
- Muscle weakness or cramping after training may indicate that the muscle was substantially affected by the training stimulus. [k:4f6b57f0]
- Muscle soreness can follow productive training, but prolonged soreness may indicate excessive stimulus and damage. [k:76bf778c]
