# Diet phases and rates

Use these facts as evidence context. Do not turn a source fact into an individualized prescription.

- Calorie tables and equations are starting estimates; tracking body weight over time helps assess whether intake is producing weight gain. [k:2b441949]
- An intake that produces very rapid weight gain, such as 15 pounds in a week and a half, is described as an overestimate likely to produce excessive fat gain. [k:5a801f2b]
- A hypercaloric intake is defined as more calories than needed to maintain body weight, and body weight is expected to rise over the long term. [k:6d07da3d]
- For an initial calorie estimate from a table or equation, the speaker suggests adding 10 percent as a starting approach to create a hypercaloric intake. [k:9e7bf50a]
- Individual calorie requirements may differ from table or equation estimates by 500 to 1000 calories. [k:d6a82f97]
