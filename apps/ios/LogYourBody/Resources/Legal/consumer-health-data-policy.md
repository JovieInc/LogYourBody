# Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: October 31, 2026**

This policy covers the consumer health data LogYourBody, Inc. ("LogYourBody", "we", "us") collects through the LogYourBody iPhone app and logyourbody.com. It is written for Washington's My Health My Data Act and similar state consumer health data laws, and supplements our [Privacy Policy](/privacy). Where the two differ for consumer health data, this policy applies.

## Consumer health data we collect

- **Body measurements you enter or sync:** weight, body fat percentage, lean mass and derived body-composition metrics (such as FFMI).
- **Apple Health data you allow us to read or write:** weight and body fat percentage (read and write), height and step count (read), date of birth and biological sex (read, when available).
- **Profile details used in body-composition calculations:** date of birth, height and gender.
- **Progress photos** you add to your timeline, and their capture dates. EXIF metadata is removed before storage.
- **What you tell the in-app coach chat**, which can include health context you choose to share.

We do not collect precise location to infer health status, and we do not use geofencing.

## Where it comes from

From you (what you type, photograph or say in the app) and from Apple Health, only for the data types you allow on your iPhone. You can change Apple Health permissions at any time in the Health app.

## Why we collect and use it

- To provide the service you asked for: show your measurements and photos on your private timeline, calculate body-composition estimates (labelled measured or estimated), sync your data across your devices, and answer your coach chat questions.
- To keep the service secure and working: detect fraud and abuse, and fix errors.

We do not use consumer health data for advertising.

## Who we share it with

We do not sell consumer health data. We share it only with service providers that process it for us to run the service, under contract:

- **Neon** (database hosting) and **Cloudflare R2** (progress-photo storage) store your data.
- **Jovie** (authentication) signs you in with Apple.
- The **coach chat provider** processes the messages you send in coach chat to generate replies.
- **Apple** (Apple Health on your device, and Sign in with Apple).

Error reporting (Sentry), subscription management (RevenueCat), product analytics and feature flags (Statsig) and hosting (Vercel) do not receive your measurements, photos or Apple Health data.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it. Before any other sharing of consumer health data, we will ask for your separate consent.

## Your rights

You can:

- see the consumer health data we hold about you, and the list of third parties and affiliates we have shared it with;
- delete it: in the app under Settings > Delete Account, or by emailing privacy@logyourbody.com;
- withdraw consent to collection or sharing: revoke Apple Health access in the Health app, or delete your account;
- export a copy: in the app under Settings > Export Data.

To make a request, email **privacy@logyourbody.com**. We respond within 45 days. If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision with "Appeal" in the subject line; we answer appeals within 45 days. Washington residents who are unhappy with the outcome of an appeal can contact the Washington Attorney General at https://www.atg.wa.gov/file-complaint.
